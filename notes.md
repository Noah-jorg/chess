# My Project notes
Pieces:
    White pieces will be denoted by capital letters,
such as Q for Queen, K for King, and so forth. The
symbols will be: K King, Q Queen, B Bishop, N Knight,
R Rook, P Pawn.

The Black pieces will be denoted with lowercase lettes:
k King, q Queen, b Bishop, n Knight, r Rook, p Pawn.
